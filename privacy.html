Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026
Summary: CalcNexus is designed so ordinary calculator inputs are processed in your browser. We do not require an account to use the calculators.

1. Calculator inputs

Most calculations are performed locally in your browser. The values you enter are not intentionally sent to CalcNexus servers merely to calculate a result.

2. Local storage

CalcNexus may use browser storage to remember preferences, language, currency, saved calculators, and other convenience settings. This information remains in your browser unless you clear it or your browser removes it.

3. Optional market-price requests

Some calculators may offer optional reference data such as exchange rates or gold prices. If you request such data, your browser may contact an external data provider. Availability, accuracy, and privacy practices of that provider are governed by the provider's own policies.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

CalcNexus may use essential browser storage or, if analytics or advertising services are enabled in a future version, cookies or similar technologies. Any third-party service used will process information according to its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Advertising and Pinterest

CalcNexus may be promoted through Pinterest or other platforms. We do not provide your private calculator inputs to Pinterest simply because you visit CalcNexus. If third-party advertising or analytics is added, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and their purposes.

6. External links

CalcNexus may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Children

CalcNexus is a general-purpose calculator website and is not directed specifically at children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The latest version will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided on the CalcNexus website.
